Nevets plays chess

← Nevets demos · The model runs in your browser. Each turn the board is sent to the model as text (a piece list) with every legal move as an option; it returns a probability for each move and plays the most likely one. Rules are enforced by chess.js; the move choice is the model's alone. Expect beginner-level play: it matches Stockfish's best move about 25% of the time.
